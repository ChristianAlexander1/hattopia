How to play

  • ◀ ▶ move, ▲ / Space jump, 👊 punch
  • Tap a tile to punch it. Break blocks for drops.
  • Select a block/seed in 🎒, then tap a tile to place.
  • Plant seeds on empty tiles above solid ground. Punch grown trees to harvest.
  • Plant a different seed on a growing tree to splice it — hybrids include furniture (Wood+Metal=Chest, Wood+Glow=Lamp…).
  • Place a World Lock to own a world. Wrench 🔧 players to trade or give access.
  • Punch a Door to teleport to spawn. Chat: /join WORLDNAME.
